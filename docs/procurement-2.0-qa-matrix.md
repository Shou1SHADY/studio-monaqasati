## Product bugs found by this wave (tests kept failing on purpose)

1. **A manager can approve a direct award above the cap** (G-16, R-36, WF-28). In 2.0 a direct award is an open private RFQ (`basis: "rfq"`) whose order carries `tags.directOver`; `requiredApprover` (`src/lib/procurement/po.ts:214`) routes it to the owner, but `approveOrder` (`src/lib/procurement/po-server.ts:284-289`) only enforces owner-only through `directOverCap` (`po-server.ts:239`), which tests `po.basis === "direct"`, and `approvalRefusal` (`po.ts:305`) only compares the amount with the manager limit. Failing assertion: `procurement-v2-wf-demand.test.ts` > WF-28 - the manager`s `POST /api/procurement/po/approve` answers 200, the test expects `reason: "owner_only"` and the order to stay `awaiting_approval`. Fix: `directOverCap` (or `approvalRefusal`) must also read `po.tags?.directOver`.
2. **The price-seal wording is still in the message files** (G-11, the retired words). Six keys, both languages: `Portal.Procurement.rfqd.cmp.close_now` ("Close now & unseal"), `rfqd.details.closed_early_title` ("Closed early - prices unsealed", LIVE: `src/components/procurement/rfq/RfqDetailsPanel.tsx:89`), `rfqd.log.closed_early` ("Closed early and prices unsealed", LIVE through `PoDrawer`/RFQ log labels), `rfqd.list.sealed_banner`, `rfqd.list.sealed_until`, `rfqpo.form.sealed_until` (the last three and `close_now` are unused). Failing assertions: `procurement-v2-names.test.ts` > "the words the package retired do not come back" (English and Arabic).
